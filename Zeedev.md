## Brand direction

Zeedev represents: technical competence, independence, creativity, confidence, thoughtful problem solving, attention to detail, capability, and practical, well-built digital products.

The site should feel sharp, peaceful, and calm at the same time: precise and confident rather than soft or decorative, but never cold or aggressive. When someone lands on it, the goal is for them to immediately think "this person is capable, and can take this job on."

Do not use generic portfolio templates, stock photography, vague marketing language, or unnecessary decoration. No em dashes anywhere in visible website copy — use commas, periods, colons, parentheses, or normal hyphens instead.

Avoid vague phrases such as "turning ideas into reality," "passionate developer," "innovative solutions," "welcome to my portfolio," "creating digital magic," "taking your business to the next level." Replace generic statements with specific, human, confident copy.

## Logo and visual identity

**Build order note:** a coding agent generating logo shapes directly in CSS/SVG from a text description tends to produce something generic. Treat the logo as a separate design step: commission or generate the mark first (designer, Canva, or an AI image tool), review it until it matches the direction below, export it as an asset, and only then hand it to the coding agent to integrate into the header, footer, and favicon. Do not ask the coding agent to invent the mark purely from this text spec.

Logo direction: sharp and geometric rather than soft or curved. Peaceful and calm in feeling, not aggressive or loud. An abstract mark built from the letters Z and D, using clean angular or precise geometric lines rather than the rounded, delicate curves of a typical "feminine" logo. The mark should read as confident and exact, the kind of symbol used by someone who solves technical problems precisely.

The logo must not use: generic female silhouettes, crowns, butterflies, flowers, generic infinity symbols, basic computer icons, cursors, circuit boards, obvious angle brackets, generic code symbols, or any design that imitates an existing company logo.

Create these variations: primary horizontal logo (symbol + zeedev wordmark), compact symbol-only mark, ZDEV compact mark, a version for dark backgrounds, a version for light backgrounds, a monochrome version, and a small favicon version. It must stay recognizable at small sizes.

Use lowercase "zeedev" for the primary wordmark and "ZDEV" as the secondary compact mark, exactly as specified, consistently across every instance in the codebase (header, footer, favicon alt text, meta tags).

## Color palette

Drop the earlier warm ivory / dusty rose / muted plum palette entirely — it read as soft and generic rather than sharp. Use a tighter, more confident system instead:

- Near Black: #0E1113 — dark section backgrounds, header/footer on the dark variant, primary text on light sections
- Warm Off-White: #F7F7F4 — light section backgrounds
- Slate Grey: #5A5F66 — secondary/supporting text, borders on light sections
- Sharp Accent, Deep Emerald: #0F7A5C — links, buttons, logo accent, highlighted numerals and tags

This is intentionally a small palette. Mix dark sections (near-black background, off-white text, emerald accents) with light sections (off-white background, near-black text, emerald accents) across the page rather than committing to one mode throughout — for example: dark hero, light about/skills, dark services or stats band, light portfolio, dark contact/footer. Keep contrast strong in both modes and avoid heavy gradients or glassmorphism. No neon colors, no pastel, no low-contrast text.

## Typography

Use a sharp, geometric sans-serif for headings (something like Space Grotesk, General Sans, or Satoshi) and a highly readable sans-serif for body text and forms (something like Inter or General Sans). No serif, no script, no decorative display font this time — the point is precision, not softness.

Comfortable font sizes, readable line lengths, generous line height on body text; headings can run tighter and larger for impact.
